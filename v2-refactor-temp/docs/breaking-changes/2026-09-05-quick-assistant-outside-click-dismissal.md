## What changed

On macOS, unpinned Quick Assistant closes when you click outside it. Opening another launcher or showing the main window leaves it open.

## Why this matters to the user

Using another floating launcher no longer dismisses your conversation. Pinned windows continue to stay open after outside clicks.

## What the user should do

Nothing — automatic.
